Sushmitha earned her Master’s in Biomedical Sciences from Rutgers School of Biomedical and Health Sciences. Her research experience spans from investigating how sickness alters social behavior to exploring the complexities of immune responses. With scientific interests at the intersection of neurobiology and immunology, Sushmitha is a curiosity-driven researcher passionate about making a meaningful impact through science. She is currently working on RNA tagging strategies to study RNA interactions. She also enjoys adventures with friends and finds joy in creative outlets like painting, baking, and dancing. A devoted animal lover, she has a special fondness for dogs.
