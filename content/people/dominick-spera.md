Dom received his Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry and Mathematical Sciences from The University of Scranton in 2024. At Scranton, he investigated reversible protein disulfide bond formation in the brain working in Dr. Tim Foley’s lab. In Fall 2024, he began his doctoral studies in the Molecular Biosciences PhD program at Rutgers University. In the Serebrenik lab, he is studying protein function using induced protein proximity. Outside of the lab, Dom enjoys playing games and spending time with family.
