Rahul received his bachelor’s and master’s degrees in biotechnology from Amity University, India, in 2022. Following graduation, he joined as a Research Fellow at the Dept. of Biochemistry, A.I.I.M.S, India under the guidance of Dr. Bela Goyal, where he investigated molecular biomarkers of cancer-related inflammation in gallbladder cancer. In 2023, he began his doctoral studies in the Molecular Biosciences PhD program at Rutgers. Beyond his research, Rahul enjoys traveling and engaging in outdoor activities and photography.
